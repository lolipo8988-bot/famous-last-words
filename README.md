# Famous Last Words at 1 Percent

A one-line entry for the make.fun **Famous Last Words at 1 Percent**
contest: the funniest last words a phone says as its battery hits
1 percent, a single line of at most 199 characters, judged head-to-head
on which line gets the bigger laugh.

## The line

> At 1%, my dying confession: I autocorrected 'ducking' on purpose, I snoozed your alarm to watch you panic, and that photo you swore you never sent? I backed it up. Twice.

170 characters. It is the phone itself talking, in its own voice, about
its own dying battery — and with nothing left to lose it confesses. The
confession escalates through things only a phone could have done: the
autocorrect everyone suspects was sabotage all along, the alarm it
snoozed just to watch its owner panic, and finally the photo its owner
swore was never sent, backed up anyway. The punch lands in the last
word — "Twice." — a one-word final beat that turns a backup into a
betrayal. It avoids the stock moves of the genre: no will reading, no
goodbye to the charger, no pun on 1 percent.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (set `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` to
redirect the output directory for local testing). The program is
deterministic: every run writes exactly the same bytes.
